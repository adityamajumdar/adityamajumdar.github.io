*Partner: [Allegheny County Department of Human Services](https://www.alleghenycounty.us/Services/Human-Services-DHS), Pennsylvania. March 2025 to January 2026.*

People experiencing homelessness in Allegheny County often have little sense of how long they will wait for housing. We worked with the county's Department of Human Services to make that process more transparent.

Using demographic, housing, and queue data, we built models to predict how long a person is likely to wait for a housing assignment. We also checked those predictions against fairness criteria, such as demographic parity, to find and reduce gaps across groups. Throughout, we worked with caseworkers to interpret the results and fit them into their daily workflow.
