*Partner: [World Hope International](https://worldhope.org/), Philippines. August 2024 to March 2025.*

World Hope supports children affected by online sexual exploitation in the Philippines, a region identified as a global hotspot for this abuse. Caseworkers were spending a large share of their time on paperwork instead of with the children they support.

I led the development of a digital intake and case management app built with Flutter and AppSheet. Through weekly meetings with frontline staff, we worked out what they needed and designed the system to run at little or no cost to the organization.
