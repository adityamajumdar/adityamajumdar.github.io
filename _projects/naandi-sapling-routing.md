*Partner: [Naandi Foundation](https://naandi.org/), India. In progress.*

The Naandi Foundation supports smallholder farmers moving to restorative agriculture, which includes delivering saplings to farms spread across rural regions. Those deliveries are currently planned by hand.

We are formulating the problem as a vehicle routing problem with real-world constraints and solving it with mixed-integer programming and faster heuristics, using Naandi's geospatial field data. The goal is to cut travel time and cost while reaching more farmers. We work closely with Naandi staff so the routes reflect conditions on the ground.

Paper in preparation: *From Manual Planning to Automated Routing: Scalable Sapling Distribution for Smallholder Farmers in India*, with Firdaus Choudhury, Arunesh Sinha, and Amulya Yadav.
